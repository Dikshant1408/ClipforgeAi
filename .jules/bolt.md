## 2024-05-18 - SQLite Sequential Scans in Video Pipeline
**Learning:** In pipelines dealing with an ever-growing queue (like video records processing), queries filtering on state (`status`) and sorting by time (`discovered_at`) without a compound index cause full table scans. Over time, as completed/failed videos accumulate in the database, UI load times and polling execution degrades significantly. Specifically, `has_channel_videos` checking for seed channels and `next_in_status` checking for pipeline advances became bottlenecks.
**Action:** Always add compound indices for frequently queried combinations of status and timestamp (`CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS idx_status_discovered_at ON videos(status, discovered_at)`) and targeted queries (`CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS idx_channel_id ON videos(channel_id)`).
