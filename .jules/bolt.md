## 2024-05-18 - Audio Window Energy LRU Caching
**Learning:** When calculating properties like audio energy (RMS) over hundreds of overlapping sliding windows (e.g. for highlight detection), repeatedly seeking and reading from disk is a massive I/O bottleneck. Moreover, Python-level slicing on a cached array can still be slow. Pre-computing cumulative sums (`np.cumsum`) over large chunks allows O(1) query time for window integrals, resulting in order-of-magnitude speedups. Always use `float64` for cumulative sums of squares to prevent severe precision loss or overflow. Keep `lru_cache` maxsize small (e.g., 8) to bound memory.
**Action:** When implementing overlapping window aggregations, chunk the data and precompute prefix sums (cumulative sums) to enable O(1) interval queries. Ensure proper `float64` typing for aggregations.
